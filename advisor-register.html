Mandatory Orientation Webinar

All applicants must attend the ICEC Orientation Webinar before activation of their Advisor ID.

The webinar covers:

  • Study Abroad Opportunities
  • Australian Skill Shortage Career Pathways
  • Advisor Incentive Structure
  • Student Recruitment Process
  • Candidate Registration Procedures
  • Sub-Advisor Programme
  • ICEC Portal Training

Who Can Become an Advisor?

  • Students
  • Teachers & Professors
  • Career Counsellors
  • HR Professionals
  • Retired Professionals
  • Business Owners
  • Community Leaders
  • Social Influencers

Advisor Earnings

  • ₹500 for every candidate who completes counselling and submits documents for a detailed Study Abroad & Career Pathway Assessment.
  • ₹10,000 – ₹20,000 per successful admission after fee payment and expiry of the applicable fee refund period.
  • Additional incentives on accommodation, education loans, travel services and other support services arranged through ICEC and its partners.

Principal Advisor & Sub-Advisor Programme

  • Recruit and mentor other advisors.
  • ₹250 for every eligible counselling case generated by your Sub-Advisor.
  • ₹3,000 for every successful admission generated by your Sub-Advisor.
  • Build your own advisor network and earn recurring incentives.

Candidate Registration Rules

  • All candidates must be registered through the ICEC system.
  • Candidate ownership is established after ICEC verification.
  • Only verified and genuine candidates qualify for incentives.
  • Repeated submission of non-genuine or unverifiable candidates may result in suspension or termination of advisor privileges.
