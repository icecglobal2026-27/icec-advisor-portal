  • Special rewards for high-performing advisors.

How the Process Works

  • Register as an ICEC Advisor.
  • Your application is reviewed by ICEC.
  • Approved advisors receive an Advisor ID.
  • Use your Advisor ID while registering candidates.
  • Track referrals and earn incentives on successful outcomes.

Important Rules

  • All candidates must be registered through official ICEC systems.
  • Advisor ID must be entered while registering candidates.
  • Only genuine and verifiable candidates qualify for incentives.
  • ICEC reserves the right to verify all candidate information.
  • Repeated submission of non-genuine leads may result in termination of advisor privileges.
